# T03

## Data Story

### Audience
The target audience for this visualisation is Australian consumers who are planning to purchase a television. This includes individuals and families comparing different TV models, screen sizes and power consumption before making a purchasing decision.

### Audience Interest
The audience is interested in choosing affordable televisions with high energy efficiency and suitable sizes that meet their needs and preferences. The visualisation helps consumers compare available options and make more informed purchasing decisions based on these factors.

## About the data

### Data source
The dataset used in this visualisation was obtained from the Australian Government's Energy Rating Data for household appliances - Labelled Products dataset. The dataset contains information about television products, including brand, model number, market region, energy consumption and other product specifications.

Source: https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/energy-rating-for-household-appliances/resource/93a615e5-935e-4713-a4b0-379e3f6dedc9

Accessed: 11 September 2026

### Data processing
The original dataset contains multiple attributes related to television products. For this visualisation, only eight relevant attributes were selected for analysis:
-Submit_ID
-Brand_Reg
-Model_No
-SoldIn
-screensize
-Screen_Tech
-Avg_mode_power
-Star Rating Index

The data was cleaned and prepared before visualisation. Record with missing values were removed where necessary. The chosen attributes were used to compare television models based on screen size, display technology, power consumption and ratings.

### Privacy
The dataset does not contain any personally identifiable information (PII). All records relate to television products and their energy performance characteristics. Therefore, no personal privacy concerns are associated with the use of this dataset.

### Accuracy and Limitations
The dataset is sourced from an official Australian Government data repository and is considered reliable. However, some records may contain missing values or become outdated as new television products enter the market. The visualisation is based on selected attributes and may not reflect all factors that influence consumer purchasing decisions.

### Ethics
The visualisation was created to help consumers compare different television models based on their ratings, power consumption and screen sizes. The data is presented as accurately as possible based on the dataset used. The visualisation is intended for educational purposes and should only be used as a guide when comparing TV products.
